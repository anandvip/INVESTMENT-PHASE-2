The IBN card is a marketed ranking. A figure is kept below only if a named page gave a number, a date, and a plan. Where two pages disagree, both numbers stay and the cell is marked doubtful. I have not opened the AMFI NAV file myself. That is the manual pass still to do.

What the card claimed

Screenshot dated 6 October 2026, 10:04. Brand line: IBN / Invest Better Now. Headline: 17 of 28 SIFs positive over the last 3 months. No as-of date, no Direct or Regular, no ARN, no benchmark.

The product class is real. Specialised Investment Funds sit under the SEBI mutual-fund regulations. Operational circular SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-I POD-1/P/CIR/2025/26, 27 February 2025, effective 1 April 2025. Minimum ₹10 lakh aggregated across strategies of one AMC at PAN level. Accredited investors exempt. Unhedged short via derivatives capped at 25% of net assets.

The two sessions that move the story

MeraSIF data note, 4 September 2026: on official AMFI NAVs, qSIF Hybrid rose 6.32% and qSIF Active Asset Allocator rose 5.33% in one day. Median SIF that day: +0.02%. Both rose again on 8 September, +2.61% and +2.12%. Any 3-month window that includes those dates includes them.

I do not have an AMC factsheet that explains those two days. Until that note is read, the +21% and +22% lines are real NAV changes and an unexplained process. Doubtful cause

How I am using this

  1. A 3-month NAV rise is a fact about a window. It is not evidence the process repeats. Most of these strategies are under a year old. Since-inception here is NAV against face value, not a CAGR.
  2. Scale and rank point different ways. Altiva Hybrid holds about ₹9,268 Cr and is +1.27% over three months. The +21% allocator is about ₹167 Cr and is an interval scheme.
  3. Cost does not explain the outliers. Arudha Hybrid at 0.63% and RedHex at 0.93% are the cheaper lines. Neither is the performance outlier.
  4. Five of the seventeen card names have the wrong sign on the 1 October Regular cut. The card is not a current screen.
  5. The ₹10 lakh floor is per PAN per AMC, across that AMC’s SIF strategies. A redemption that I initiate can force the rest out if the floor breaks. A fall caused only by NAV does not.

Nothing above is an instruction to buy, sell, or ignore a scheme.
